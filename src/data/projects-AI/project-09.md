### Description

BalaEMS is an event attendance tracking system built for Bala Events. Administrators create and manage events and staff accounts from a protected dashboard, guests register through a public form and receive a PDF ticket with a QR code, and employees use a mobile QR scanner for real-time check-in. Role-based access with JWT authentication keeps admin, staff and public areas separate.
