### Description

The Ahaz Attendance System combines a web dashboard with a mobile app. Employees check in and out from their phones, validated by geofencing, office Wi-Fi and QR codes. Administrators manage employees and registrations, track daily attendance, handle job applicants, configure office settings and generate attendance and payroll reports.
