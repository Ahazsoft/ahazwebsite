### Description

Yuyana CRM & Dashboard is a pair of web applications built for Yuyana Tour & Travel. The CRM covers accounts, leads, opportunities, projects, invoicing, document storage and reporting, and the dashboard manages the tours, destinations, gallery and appointments shown on the public travel website.
